## How It Works
RazorpayX Payout Links removes the friction, time, and effort required to collect bank account details to make payouts. Just enter the beneficiary’s contact details and we do the rest - from sending the link to collect bank account/UPI details, processing the payout and giving you the confirmation!
Step 1
- Create a Payout Link via API or Dashboard
Step 2
- Share the Payout Link over SMS, Email etc.
Step 3
- Beneficiary enters preferred bank account / UPI ID.
Step 4
- Payout instantly processed to beneficiary account

# Startups

## What this page is
<!-- ai:begin method=ai — written by the describe step, NOT ground truth -->
Razorpay Startup Perks: a benefits programme offering up to ₹1 crore in credits and discounts to funded startups, gated by funding stage rather than sold as a product.

### Purpose
Acquire startups early by giving away benefits, on the bet they grow into payments customers. It splits its audience by funding stage and routes each to custom pricing.

### Location
Path `/m/startup-perks/`, reached from the Startups item in the primary nav and from the homepage. The `/m/` prefix marks it as a campaign or microsite path rather than a product page.

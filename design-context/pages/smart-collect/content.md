# Smart Collect 2.0

### Instant Collections & Automated Reconciliation- Redefine Your Bank Transfers
Automatically reconcile incoming UPI, IMPS, NEFT and RTGS payments and enjoy real-time, instant collections that keep your cash flow agile.

### Collect Smarter, Not Harder How does RazorpayX Smart Collect 2.0 work?

#### Setup & Integration
Get
unique identifier series
linked to your business bank account*
Integrate with RazorpayX and create identifiers using our APIs/Dashboard

#### Streamline your Collections
Share these identifiers with your customers
Customers can start making
UPI, IMPS, NEFT or RTGS
payments to these identifiers

#### Monitor & Reconcile
Funds land
instantly in your bank account
Track and reconcile all payments in real-time via dashboard and instant webhooks
*For unique use cases, we offer customer identifiers via the Razorpay Payment Gateway, which has limited features.

Seamlessly refund any payment made to

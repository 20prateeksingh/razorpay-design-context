# Engage

## What this page is
<!-- ai:begin method=ai — written by the describe step, NOT ground truth -->
Razorpay Engage: a growth-marketing suite built on payments data, selling offers, gift cards and loyalty as one platform. Its call to action is "Talk to an expert", marking it as a sales-led enterprise product.

### Purpose
Sell marketing tooling to businesses that already take payments through Razorpay, on the argument that payment data is better targeting data. Structured around three verbs: Acquire, Retain, Revive.
